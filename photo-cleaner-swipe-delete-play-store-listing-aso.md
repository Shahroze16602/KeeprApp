# Photo Cleaner: Swipe & Delete Google Play listing and ASO plan

Research date: 2026-08-16
Primary market/language: English (United States)

## Recommended main store listing

### App name — 29/30 characters

Photo Cleaner: Swipe & Delete

### Short description — 70/80 characters

Swipe to delete photos, clean your gallery, and free up storage safely

### Full description

Too many photos and never enough time to sort them? Photo Cleaner: Swipe & Delete is a swipe photo cleaner that turns camera roll cleanup into a quick, satisfying routine. Choose a month, review photos one by one, and decide what stays or goes at your own pace.

SWIPE TO CLEAN YOUR GALLERY

Swipe right to keep a photo and left to mark it for deletion. Prefer buttons? Use the clearly labeled Keep and Delete controls. If you change your mind, undo your last choice before deletion begins.

CLEAN PHOTOS MONTH BY MONTH

Break a crowded camera roll into manageable monthly sessions. Start with any accessible month, leave whenever you need to, and resume from your saved progress later. You can also clean only the photos you select through Android.

REVIEW BEFORE YOU DELETE

A swipe never removes a photo immediately. Photo Cleaner: Swipe & Delete separates sorting from deletion so you can review both lists, move photos between Keep and Delete, or start over. Deletion begins only after you confirm in Photo Cleaner: Swipe & Delete and approve Android's system prompt.

FREE UP STORAGE WITH CONFIDENCE

See each photo's date and available file size while sorting. After cleanup, Photo Cleaner: Swipe & Delete reports the photos deleted, the memories kept, unresolved items, and the estimated storage reclaimed. Nothing is counted as deleted unless Photo Cleaner: Swipe & Delete verifies that it is gone.

MAKE CLEANUP FEEL ACHIEVABLE

Earn XP, build a cleanup streak, clear months, and watch your reclaimed storage grow. Optional haptics, motion controls, and light, dark, or system themes let you make the experience your own.

PRIVATE PHOTO CLEANUP

Your photo content is processed on your device and is not uploaded by Photo Cleaner: Swipe & Delete. The app only reads photos Android allows it to access, and no account is required. You stay in control of every keep or delete decision.

KEY FEATURES

- Swipe right to keep and left to delete
- Visible Keep and Delete buttons
- Undo before deletion
- Month-by-month photo cleanup
- Selected-photo cleanup on supported Android versions
- Saved sessions and progress
- Final Keep and Delete review
- Android system deletion confirmation
- Reclaimed-storage and cleanup statistics
- XP, levels, streaks, and month milestones
- Light, dark, and system themes

Use Photo Cleaner: Swipe & Delete as your photo cleaner, gallery cleaner, camera roll cleaner, and swipe-to-delete companion. Review old screenshots, blurry shots, repeated takes, and forgotten photos yourself—then keep the memories that matter.

Demand and competition are directional, based on current Google Play titles, descriptions, review counts, and download bands. Exact search-volume scores require Play Console query data or a paid ASO dataset. After launch, replace assumptions with the search terms and conversion data reported for Photo Cleaner: Swipe & Delete.

### Keywords to avoid

Do not target these until the product actually supports them:

- duplicate photo remover or duplicate detector
- AI photo cleaner, smart cleaner, or blurry-photo detector
- video cleaner or video compressor
- album organizer, folders, or photo tagging
- secret gallery, vault, or photo recovery
- automatic cleanup or one-tap cleanup

These terms may have demand, but using them now would attract the wrong users and create a misleading listing.

## Creative asset brief

### Screenshot order and overlay copy

Use real release-build UI, minimal overlay text, and the same visual grammar as the app. The first three screenshots matter most because they communicate the product before a visitor expands the description.

1. **Clean your camera roll, one month at a time**
   Show the populated month picker and progress card.
2. **Swipe right to keep. Left to delete.**
   Show the photo card mid-swipe with clear Keep/Delete semantics.
3. **Review every choice before deletion**
   Show the final Keep and Delete review screen.
4. **Undo mistakes before anything is removed**
   Show the cleanup screen with Undo visible.
5. **Android confirms what gets deleted**
   Show the in-app confirmation state; do not fabricate or heavily edit the Android system dialog.
6. **See the storage you reclaimed**
   Show the completion screen with kept, deleted, unresolved, and space-reclaimed stats.
7. **Your photos stay on your device**
   Show the photo-access/privacy explanation screen.
8. **Build streaks. Clear the backlog.**
   Show levels, streaks, and cleared-month progress.

Do not upload the design-system prototype screenshots as store assets: several contain prototype controls or obsolete monetization copy. Capture clean screenshots from the exact release candidate. Use licensed or purpose-made sample photos with no personal information.

### Feature graphic

- Required size: 1024 × 500 px, JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha.
- Concept: cream background, one oversized tilted photo-card stack, green Keep and coral Delete accents, and the line **Your camera roll. Finally finished.**
- Keep the center and outer edges visually simple so cropping remains safe.
- Do not add “Download,” “Install,” “Best,” “#1,” pricing, ratings, awards, or excessive keyword text.

### Icon guidance

Keep the current stacked-card/checkmark idea if it remains distinctive at small sizes. A/B test only meaningful alternatives: current icon versus a simpler high-contrast card/checkmark with fewer internal details. Do not add text, stars, ranking badges, or notification dots.

## Play Console setup

- App type: App
- Category: Photography. Google defines this category as including photo-management tools.
- Tags: choose up to five only from Play Console's currently suggested list, prioritizing the most literal photo-management and gallery-related options. Do not force unrelated high-traffic tags.
- Default language: English (United States)
- Contact email: required; use a domain-based support address before launch if possible.
- Privacy policy: use the published HTTPS version of the reviewed Photo Cleaner: Swipe & Delete privacy policy.
- Ads and in-app purchases declarations: match the exact release configuration, not the intended future business model.

## First ASO experiments

Run experiments only after enough store traffic is available to reach a useful result.

1. Test screenshot 1 message: **Clean your camera roll, one month at a time** versus **Swipe through photo clutter in minutes**.
2. Test short description:
   - Control: **Swipe to delete photos, clean your gallery, and free up storage safely**
   - Variant: **Clean photos month by month. Swipe, review, and free up storage safely**
3. Test the current icon against one simplified icon—not multiple visual changes at once.
4. Test the feature graphic after screenshots and icon, because screenshots usually have more direct conversion impact for apps.

Run one asset variable at a time and keep each test active for at least one full week. Judge variants by first-time installer conversion and retained installers, not just listing visits.

Once the default listing has data, create search-keyword custom store listings for distinct intents:

- **photo cleaner / gallery cleaner:** lead with quick visual cleanup
- **free up storage:** lead with reclaimed-space results
- **swipe to delete photos:** lead with the gesture and review flow
- **private photo cleaner:** lead with on-device photo content and Android-controlled access

## Organic growth plan after launch

- Track ranking and conversion weekly for the keyword clusters above. Avoid reacting to daily noise.
- Read new reviews by theme: ads/paywalls, deletion trust, speed, crashes, missing media, and resume behavior.
- Ask for a rating only after a successfully completed cleanup. Never gate functionality or reward a particular rating.
- Keep crash/ANR rate and cleanup-session completion healthy; weak product quality will suppress sustainable organic growth even if metadata earns impressions.
- Localize the app experience, listing text, and screenshot overlays together. Start with Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, German, and French only after the shipping UI is genuinely translated.
- Use a small crawlable product page targeting the same honest use cases—photo cleaner, month-by-month cleanup, and review-before-delete—and link directly to Google Play.
- Refresh screenshots when the UI changes; do not change proven metadata without a hypothesis and measurement window.

## Release issues to resolve before publishing

1. **Ads/billing versus product promise:** the current project includes AdMob, billing, RevenueCat, premium screens, and remote-config switches even though defaults are off and the design PRD promises permanent free access. The listing above deliberately makes no “ad-free,” “no subscription,” or “unlimited” claim. If those are meant to be permanent differentiators, remove the runtime monetization path and SDK behavior before adding the claims.
2. **Language claim:** the in-app language chooser lists ten languages, but core Photo Cleaner: Swipe & Delete UI copy is hard-coded in English and no localized Android string resources are present. Publish only an English listing until localization is complete.
3. **Generic-title differentiation:** category-led names are crowded. Make this listing recognizable through a distinctive icon, consistent cream/green/coral visuals, and the review-before-delete promise; do not imitate Swipewipe's icon, screenshots, or wording.
4. **Data Safety consistency:** Firebase Remote Config, possible analytics, advertising, billing, and RevenueCat behavior must be reflected consistently in the Data Safety form, ads declaration, privacy policy, and consent flow for the exact release bundle.
5. **Listing truthfulness:** do not add duplicate detection, AI cleanup, video cleanup, favorites, album sorting, offline-only, or zero-data-collection claims without verifying those capabilities in the release candidate.
